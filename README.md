### About

I'm a software engineering student who learns primarily through building. I take ideas, turn them into working systems, break them, debug them, and ship them. My work sits at the intersection of **software engineering**, **Web3**, and **systems programming** — with a growing interest in **AI** and **cybersecurity**.

I'm currently focused on smart contract development on **Stellar/Soroban** using **Rust**, and building full-stack applications that solve real problems.
